Workflow Showcase
Clipboard History & Bookmarks
From installation to one-click re-use — your clipboard, always under control.
1
Install the Module
Search Clipboard History Manager in the Odoo Apps store and click Module Info to install. Available as a standalone module — no dependencies required.
2
Clipboard History Panel
Click the Clipboard icon in the top bar to open the side panel. The History tab shows all copied items with timestamps. Bookmarked items are highlighted with a ★ star.
3
View Clipboard History
The History tab tooltip confirms "View clipboard history". All captured items are listed newest-first with precise copy timestamps for full traceability.
4
Smart Search & Filter
Type in the search bar to instantly filter history. Results show a result count (e.g. "3 results found") and only matching items are displayed — making retrieval instant even with large histories.
5
1-Click Re-Copy
Hover any history item to reveal action buttons. Click the Copy icon ("Copy to clipboard") to instantly put that text back on your clipboard — ready to paste anywhere in Odoo.
6
Bookmark & Remove Bookmark
Click the ★ Star button to bookmark any item for permanent access. The tooltip shows "Remove bookmark" when active — toggling bookmarks is instant and persistent across sessions.
7
Delete Items
Click the 🗑️ Delete icon ("Delete item") to remove any entry from history permanently. Keep your clipboard list clean and relevant — delete one item or clear all at once.
8
Bookmarks Tab
Switch to Bookmarks tab for a dedicated view of all starred items. Only bookmarked entries appear here — your personal shortlist of frequently reused text, always at hand.
9
Settings & Premium Features
The Settings tab lets you enable/disable the Clipboard Manager and Notifications toggles. Premium features include auto-capture, bookmark management, smart search, copy frequency tracking, and customizable notifications.
